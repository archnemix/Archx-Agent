Wipe data
Every action asks for confirmation first and writes through the crash-safe queue. The fixed manifest is never touched.

            

              
Wipe everythingProviders, encrypted keys, models, profiles, settings, chats, logs, assets — full reset, then reload.

              
Wipe chat data, keep providers & personalizationDeletes conversations, request logs and memories. Providers, encrypted keys, models, profiles, your name/agent name and all settings stay.

              
Wipe chats onlyConversations and their messages disappear; keys, settings and generated artifacts remain.

              
Wipe image assetsDeletes stored image files and their asset records.

              
Wipe video assetsDeletes stored video files and their asset records.

              
Wipe document assetsDeletes stored documents/audio and their asset records.

              
Wipe the library fullyEvery asset of every kind plus the media folder contents.
